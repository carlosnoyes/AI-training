Challenge 1 - Writing

Speaker Intro: Save the Event

Scenario

You are hosting an AI event at work. The next speaker, Carlos Noyes, is about to give a talk on the importance of AI-training, but he never sent his intro. Generate an accurate, engaging introduction for him.

Hints
  • The AI does not know much about Carlos Noyes. What is the best way to tell it?
  • Try using his LinkedIn profile as context for the AI.

Prompt Template: Intro Generator

Bad Solution

Write me a speaker introduction for Carlos Noyes.

Good Solution
Context: Carlos Noyes is about to speak on the importance of AI-training. Instructions: Using his LinkedIn page, write a 60-90 second speaker introduction for him. LinkedIn Profile: [Paste full LinkedIn profile text]
Compare Results

Show outputs from both prompts to highlight the difference in accuracy, tone, and specificity.

Challenge 1 - Lesson

Writing Quality = Prompt + Context Quality

Great writing outputs come from clear objectives, audience, constraints, and structure.
Raw context dumps are easy and often effective. Start with the most direct source of context and refine if needed.
The core skill: equip the model the way you would equip a new intern for success.

Challenge 2 - Reasoning

Trade Show Turnaround: 50 Qualified Leads

Scenario

You lead your company's annual trade show booth. Historically, the value from this event has been limited and hard to measure. The trade show is in 30 days. You want it to result in 50+ qualified leads. What is your plan?

Hints
  • Remember to give the AI relevant background and context to perform this task.
  • Break into sub-steps. Start broad (ideation), then select and deepen. Iterate rather than asking once.
  • Consider adding the phrase "Think very long and hard" to your instructions to increase output quality.
Slide 4 of 15
Challenge 2 - Solution

Prompt Chain: Context - Ideas - Full Plans

Prompt 1 (Context)
Summarize everything important about the company I work for: [paste website text]
Prompt 2 (Ideation)
I have 30 days to prepare for a trade show to maximize qualified leads. Generate 10 creative tactics that I could employ this year.
Prompt 3 (Planning)
I like ideas X/Y/Z. Think very long and hard and create a detailed plan for each: prep steps, execution steps, materials, staffing, cost breakdown, etc.
Slide 5 of 15
Challenge 2 - Lesson

AI for Strategy: Decompose + Iterate

Treat AI as a strategic partner: it is strongest when you guide it through stages.
Technique: decomposition (context - ideation - selection - execution) plus iteration.
If you supply unstructured context, ask it to summarize first so the chat has a clean reference.
"Think very long and hard" tricks reasoning models into allocating more computational resources.

Challenge 3 - Lesson

Research Acceleration Requires Verification

AI can provide general and publicly accessible information.
If answers may have changed recently, add "Do a web search for up to date information."
For niche questions, give it a method or source to search (for example, "most data is in this report").
When tasks are large, break them up and use "Think very long and hard" to increase intelligence.
Two key techniques: multimodal context (screenshots help) and explicit self-check instructions.

Challenge 4 - Analysis

Is the Renovation Worth It?

Scenario

You are selling your house (2bd/1ba).
You could pay to add an extra bed and bath to increase its value.
Before commiting to the renovation, you want to know the average price per sqft in your zip code for 2bd/1ba and 3bd/2ba using Zillow data.

Hints
  • AI can structure messy data (for example, text pasted from a web page).
  • Excel or Sheets is great for running calculations if you know the right formulas.
  • Try different models if you are running into issues.
Slide 10 of 15
Challenge 4 - Solution

Workflow: Extract - Table - Sheets Formulas

Step 1 - AI
Put the following into a table with headers: price, sqft, bd, ba: [Paste Zillow text]
Step 2 - Repeat

Do this for each page or listing batch.

Step 3 - Sheets
I pasted the table into Google Sheets. Can you give me the formula I need to calculate the average price per sqft for all 2bd/1ba homes? And the formula for 3bd/2ba homes?
Slide 11 of 15
Challenge 4 - Lesson

AI Bridges Messy Data - Real Analysis

AI is excellent at structuring and reformatting messy text into usable tables.
Instant models can outperform reasoning models on long, monotonous reformatting tasks.
If a model returns an answer without showing its work, validate with transparent tools.
Reasoning models are generally more reliable when they show "Thinking" or "Analyzing".
Best practice: do calculations in Sheets, Excel, or Python so results are auditable.

Challenge 5 - Multiple

Standardize Sales Prep with AI

Scenario

Your sales team's pre-call research is inconsistent. Build a repeatable ChatGPT prompt flow that organizes internal info, pulls relevant web context, and produces a skimmable prep brief.

Hints
  • What sources of relevant information do you have?
  • What is an easy-to-follow process for the user to provide that to the AI?
  • Most AIs can do some amount of web searching to gather more information.
  • What would be helpful information for a salesperson to review?
Slide 13 of 15
Challenge 5 - Solution

3-Prompt Flow: Profile - Web - Brief

Prompt 1 - Internal synthesis
Please summarize everything of relevance from a potential sales lead of mine: His LinkedIn page text: [Paste raw LinkedIn page] His company's web page: [Paste web page] Notes I have on him: [Any notes you have]
Prompt 2 - Web research
Think very long and hard, and search the web for any relevant information (leadership, funding, trends, partnerships, interviews, etc.) related to my lead or his company.
Prompt 3 - Generate guide
(Optional but powerful) Here is a template sales script for you to update. [Upload a pre-developed script] I am a salesperson with [your company name], trying to sell [product/service]. Think very long and hard and create a "Sales Call Preparation Guide" that includes: A) The prospect's profile: a concise summary of all relevant information from the chat history. B) Objection handling: potential objections and strategic responses tailored to the prospect. C) Leading questions: persuasive questions designed to make the prospect recognize the value.
Slide 14 of 15
Challenge 5 - Lesson

From One-Off Prompts to Repeatable Workflows

This is how you scale AI value throughout your team or organization.
Find high-value use cases and apply the right techniques to solve them.
Iterate until you are satisfied with the output quality and consistency.
Codify the workflow as the new procedure and train your team to follow it.
